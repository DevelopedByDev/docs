Getting started with Overlay takes less than five minutes. This guide walks you through creating an account, sending your first message, choosing the right model for your work, and saving something to memory so Overlay can carry context into future conversations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to getoverlay.io">
    Open [getoverlay.io](https://getoverlay.io) in your browser. You'll land on the Overlay homepage.

    Click **Get started** or **Sign in** in the top navigation. Either button takes you to the sign-in screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your account">
    On the sign-in screen, enter your email address and follow the prompts to create an account. Overlay supports email-based sign-in with no password required — you'll receive a magic link or code to verify your address.

    Once verified, you're taken directly into the Overlay workspace.

    <Note>
      No credit card is required to create an account. You start on the Free plan automatically.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start your first chat">
    When you land in the workspace, the **Chat** area is open by default. You'll see a message input at the bottom of the screen.

    Type anything — a question, a task, something you want to think through — and press **Enter** or click the send button. Overlay streams a response in real time.

    Your conversation is saved automatically. You can return to it from the chat history in the left sidebar at any time.

    <Tip>
      Not sure what to start with? Try asking Overlay to summarize a topic you're working on, draft an email, or explain a concept you've been meaning to research.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Overlay gives you access to models from multiple providers. By default, free accounts use the **Auto** model, which automatically routes to a capable free model.

    To switch models, click the model selector near the message input. A dropdown shows all available models grouped by capability and cost tier. Each model shows whether it supports vision, reasoning, or other special capabilities.

    Choose a model that fits your task:

    - For everyday questions and drafting, lighter and faster models work well.
    - For complex reasoning, analysis, or multi-step problems, pick a premium reasoning model.
    - For tasks that involve images or screenshots, choose a vision-capable model.

    <Info>
      Paid plans unlock the full model list, including premium models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, and more. Free accounts always have access to the Auto model at no cost.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Ask vs. Act mode">
    Below the message input, you'll see a mode toggle for **Ask** and **Act**.

    - **Ask mode** is the default. Use it for questions, analysis, drafting, research, and anything where you want a thoughtful response grounded in your knowledge base.
    - **Act mode** enables tool use and execution. Switch to it when you want Overlay to take a real action — like searching the web with a browser session, triggering an integration, or running an automation.

    To switch modes, click the toggle next to the message input before you send your message. You can switch modes at any point during a conversation.

    <Warning>
      Act mode can take real actions — like sending messages or modifying connected apps — depending on which integrations you have enabled. Review what tools are active before using Act mode on sensitive tasks.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save a memory">
    Overlay's Memory system lets you store facts, preferences, and standing instructions that carry forward into every future conversation.

    To save your first memory:

    1. Click **Memory** in the left sidebar navigation.
    2. Click **Add memory** or the **+** button.
    3. Type something you want Overlay to remember — your name, your role, a recurring preference, or a standing instruction like "always respond in bullet points."
    4. Save it.

    From now on, Overlay will draw on that memory when it's relevant to a conversation. You can view, edit, and delete memories at any time from the Memory section.

    <Tip>
      Good memories to start with: your name and job role, the kind of work you do most often, your preferred writing style, or any recurring context you find yourself re-explaining in chat.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's next?

Now that you've sent your first message and saved a memory, here are some things worth exploring:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Upload files to Knowledge" icon="upload">
    Add documents, PDFs, or notes to your Knowledge base so Overlay can reference them during chat without you needing to paste content each time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Create a Project" icon="folder">
    Group related chats, notes, and files into a Project to keep your work organized and keep context scoped to the right initiative.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Write a Note" icon="file-text">
    Open the Notes area and start a draft. Notes support rich text, slash commands, and autosave — and they can be linked to a project or attached to a chat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connect an Integration" icon="plug">
    Head to Integrations and connect Gmail, Notion, Google Calendar, or another app. Once connected, you can ask Overlay to take actions in those tools using Act mode.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
