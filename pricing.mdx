Overlay is free to start and stays free for core workspace use. When you're ready for premium models, browser tasks, image and video generation, and advanced agents, you upgrade to a paid plan. The paid plan is different from most AI subscriptions: instead of a fixed $20 seat, you choose how much monthly budget you actually want — starting at $8 and going up to $200.

That's a meaningful difference from ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) and Claude Pro ($20/month), both of which have a single fixed starting price. With Overlay, lighter users can start at $8 and scale up only when their usage grows.

## Plans

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free">
    The Free plan gives you full access to the Overlay workspace with no credit card required. You can chat using the **Auto** model, which automatically routes to a capable free model, and use all the core organizational features.

    **What's included:**

    - Unlimited messages with the Auto model (free model, auto-selected)
    - Chat, Notes, Knowledge, and Projects
    - Basic AI tools and core workspace flows
    - 10 MB file storage

    **What's not included:**

    - Premium models (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and others)
    - Image and video generation
    - Browser automation tasks
    - Daytona code execution sandboxes
    - Advanced agents and premium workflows

    <Note>
      The Free plan never expires and doesn't require a payment method. You can use it indefinitely and upgrade at any time.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Paid">
    The Paid plan unlocks everything in Free plus premium models, media generation, browser tasks, and more. You choose your own monthly budget — there's no single fixed price.

    **Budget options:**

    | Tier | Monthly budget | Storage | Best for |
    |------|---------------|---------|----------|
    | Starter | $8/month | 1 GB | Light monthly use |
    | Pro | $24/month | 3 GB | Regular daily work |
    | Max | $96/month | 12 GB | Heavy or team use |
    | Custom | $8–$200/month | Scales with budget | Set exactly what you need |

    All paid tiers include everything in Free, plus:

    - **Premium chat models** — Full access to GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, and more
    - **Image generation** — Multiple image models with aspect ratio control
    - **Video generation** — Text-to-video and image-to-video generation
    - **Browser tasks** — AI-controlled browser sessions for live web work
    - **Daytona sandboxes** — Run code in isolated execution environments
    - **Advanced agents and premium workflows** — Multi-step automations and full Act mode capabilities

    <Info>
      Storage scales with your budget. An $8/month plan includes 1 GB. A $96/month plan includes 12 GB. Custom budgets scale proportionally.
    </Info>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to choose your budget

The Starter tier at $8/month is designed for people who use premium AI tools a few times a week. The Pro tier at $24/month works well for daily work. The Max tier at $96/month is better suited to heavy users running frequent agents, media generation, and browser tasks.

If none of those feel right, the **Custom** option lets you drag a slider to any amount between $8 and $200 per month in $1 increments. You're not locked into a preset tier.

<Tip>
  Start with the Starter plan at $8/month and upgrade later if your usage grows. You can change your plan at any time through the billing portal in your Account settings.
</Tip>

## How billing works

Overlay's paid plan is budget-based, not seat-based.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Monthly budget" icon="wallet">
    Your subscription sets your monthly usage budget. A $24/month plan gives you $24 of usage budget per billing cycle.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Usage draws it down" icon="gauge-circle">
    Premium features consume budget as you use them — models, image generation, browser tasks, and other premium tools each have a usage cost.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Top up if needed" icon="plus-circle">
    If you run low, you can add a one-time top-up of $8 (or a larger amount from your Account settings) without changing your plan.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Top-ups

Top-ups refill your budget without changing your subscription tier. The default top-up amount is $8. You can change the top-up amount in your Account settings.

You can also enable **automatic top-ups**. When enabled, Overlay automatically adds $8 to your budget when it reaches zero, so you're never interrupted mid-session. Auto top-ups are off by default — you have to explicitly opt in, and they only apply to paid accounts.

<Warning>
  Automatic top-ups charge your payment method when your budget hits zero. Make sure your default top-up amount is set to a value you're comfortable with before enabling them.
</Warning>

## Managing your subscription

You can view and manage your subscription from your **Account** page inside Overlay. From there you can:

- View your current plan and remaining budget
- Change your monthly budget amount
- Enable or disable automatic top-ups
- Add a one-time top-up
- Update your payment method
- Download invoices

To cancel or make changes not available in the app, use the **billing portal** linked from your Account page.
