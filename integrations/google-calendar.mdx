The Google Calendar integration lets Overlay's AI read and write your calendar. You can check your upcoming schedule, create events, update existing ones, and ask about your availability — all from a conversation in Act mode.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="View upcoming events" icon="calendar">
    See what's on your schedule for today, this week, or any date range.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Create events" icon="plus-circle">
    Schedule meetings, set reminders, and add events with full details.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Update events" icon="edit">
    Change the time, title, description, or attendees of existing events.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Delete events" icon="trash-2">
    Remove events from your calendar when plans change.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Check availability" icon="clock">
    Ask whether you are free at a specific time or on a given day.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Find scheduling gaps" icon="grid">
    Let the AI identify open slots across your calendar for a given duration.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to connect Google Calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Click **Integrations** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Google Calendar">
    Locate **Google Calendar** in the Available list or search for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect**. A new tab opens with Google's OAuth screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and authorize">
    Sign in to the Google account whose calendar you want to connect. Review the permissions and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using it">
    Once authorized, switch to Act mode in any chat to query or manage your calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example prompts

Use these in Act mode to get started:

- "What's on my calendar this week?"
- "Am I free tomorrow afternoon?"
- "Schedule a meeting with John on Friday at 2 PM — call it 'Project sync' and make it 30 minutes."
- "Move my 10 AM meeting on Thursday to 11 AM."
- "Find a free 1-hour slot for me this week."
- "Delete the event called 'Team lunch' on Wednesday."
- "Show me all events I have with Sarah next month."

## Permissions required

When you connect Google Calendar, Google will ask you to grant the following access:

| Permission | Why Overlay needs it |
|---|---|
| Read calendar events | To show your schedule and check availability |
| Create and edit events | To schedule and update events on your behalf |
| Delete events | To remove events when you ask |

<Note>
  Overlay connects to the primary calendar on the Google account you authorize. Events from other calendars shared with your account may also be visible depending on your Google Calendar settings.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Deleting or modifying events through Act mode makes changes to your real calendar immediately. Double-check details before confirming any action that creates, updates, or removes an event.
</Warning>
