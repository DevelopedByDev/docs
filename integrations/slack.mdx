The Slack integration lets Overlay's AI interact with your Slack workspace. From Act mode, you can send messages to channels, read recent messages, and search across conversations — without switching tabs.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Send messages to channels" icon="send">
    Post to any channel your Slack account has access to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send direct messages" icon="message-circle">
    Send a DM to any member of your workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Read recent messages" icon="book-open">
    Retrieve the latest messages from a channel or conversation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search messages" icon="search">
    Search across your workspace by keyword, channel, or user.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Summarize conversations" icon="layers">
    Ask for a plain-language summary of recent channel activity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Draft message content" icon="edit">
    Have the AI write the message, then send it from chat.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to connect Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Click **Integrations** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Slack">
    Locate **Slack** in the Available list or search for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect**. A new tab opens with Slack's OAuth screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your workspace and authorize">
    Select the Slack workspace you want to connect. Review the requested permissions and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using it">
    Switch to Act mode in any chat to send messages or read Slack activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can only connect one Slack workspace per Overlay account. If you need to work with multiple workspaces, you will need to disconnect and reconnect to switch.
</Note>

## Example prompts

Use these in Act mode to get started:

- "Post a standup update to #team-updates: shipped the new auth flow, working on the dashboard today, no blockers."
- "Summarize the last 20 messages in #engineering."
- "Send a DM to @sarah asking if she reviewed the proposal."
- "Search Slack for any messages mentioning 'deployment issue' from this week."
- "What has been discussed in #general today?"
- "Post a message to #releases announcing that version 2.1 is now live."

## Permissions required

When you connect Slack, you will be asked to grant the following access:

| Permission | Why Overlay needs it |
|---|---|
| Send messages | To post to channels and DMs on your behalf |
| Read messages | To retrieve recent messages and channel history |
| Search messages | To find content across the workspace |
| Read workspace info | To identify channels and users when you reference them by name |

<Warning>
  Messages sent through Act mode are posted immediately and appear in your Slack workspace as coming from you. Review the content carefully before asking the AI to send.
</Warning>
