Overlay is a single browser tab that replaces a stack of disconnected AI tools. Instead of switching between ChatGPT for chat, Notion for notes, Google Drive for files, and a separate automation tool for recurring tasks, you get one workspace where every part shares context with every other part. Your memories shape your chat responses. Your notes can become knowledge. Your project files are available in every conversation scoped to that project. Nothing gets lost between sessions.

The product is model-agnostic by design. You can work with top models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Groq, and more — all from the same interface — and pick the right model for each task rather than committing to one vendor forever.

## Why Overlay exists

Most AI products make a tradeoff: the chat is good, but the context is ephemeral. You have to re-explain yourself every session. Your files live in a different tab. Your notes have no connection to your AI. Your automations are wired together with glue.

Overlay is built the opposite way. Context is persistent by design. Every conversation can draw from your saved memories, uploaded files, and project history. Every generated image or video gets stored in your Outputs library instead of disappearing into chat history. Every integration is available when you need to act, not just think.

The result feels less like "chat with AI" and more like working from an intelligent layer that knows your context and can move from idea to execution without leaving the tab.

## Ask mode vs. Act mode

Every chat in Overlay runs in one of two modes:

- **Ask** — The model synthesizes answers, analyzes content, drafts text, and retrieves information from your knowledge base. This is the default for research, writing, and thinking work.
- **Act** — The model uses tools and takes real actions: running browser tasks, triggering integrations, executing automations, and calling external APIs. Switch to Act when you want results, not just answers.

<Tip>
  You can switch between Ask and Act at any time during a conversation. Start by thinking through a problem in Ask mode, then flip to Act when you're ready to execute.
</Tip>

## Models supported

Overlay gives you access to models across all major providers, including OpenAI (GPT series), Anthropic (Claude series), Google (Gemini series), xAI (Grok), DeepSeek, Groq, and more. Free accounts are automatically routed to a capable free model. Paid accounts unlock the full model list, including premium reasoning and vision models.

<Note>
  The model list in Overlay is updated as new models are released. You don't need to configure anything — new models appear in the selector when they're available.
</Note>

## What you can build and do

Beyond chat, Overlay supports:

- **Image generation** — Generate images from prompts, choose from multiple models, control aspect ratio, and edit from a prior image.
- **Video generation** — Run text-to-video and image-to-video jobs asynchronously and track them in your Outputs library.
- **Browser automation** — Launch AI-controlled browser sessions that can navigate, inspect, and interact with live websites.
- **Scheduled automations** — Set up recurring AI workflows that run on your behalf at a cadence you choose.
- **Sandboxed code execution** — Run code in isolated environments for technical workflows and agent tasks.
