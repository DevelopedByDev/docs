Overlay includes a built-in voice recorder that turns your spoken words into text. You can capture ideas, dictate notes, summarize a meeting, or send a voice prompt into chat — all without typing. Transcription is handled by AI, so you get clean, readable text from natural speech in seconds.

## Where to find Voice

Access voice recording in two places:

- **Voice area** — Select **Voice** from the left sidebar for a dedicated recording interface with full controls and output options.
- **In-chat** — Use the microphone icon in the chat input bar to record directly into a conversation.

## Record and transcribe audio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Voice area or chat input">
    Navigate to **Voice** in the sidebar, or click the microphone icon inside the chat input bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow microphone access">
    The first time you use Voice, your browser asks for microphone permission. Click **Allow**. Overlay uses your browser's built-in microphone capture — nothing is installed on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Click the **Record** button (or the microphone icon in chat). Speak clearly into your microphone. A waveform or timer confirms the recording is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop recording">
    Click **Stop** when you're finished speaking. Overlay sends the audio to AI transcription automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the transcript">
    The transcribed text appears in seconds. Read through it and confirm it captured your words accurately before deciding what to do with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to do with the transcript

Once your transcript is ready, you have three options:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Copy to clipboard" icon="clipboard">
    Copy the transcript text to paste it anywhere — a document, email, or another app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Save as a note" icon="notebook">
    Save the transcript directly as a note in Overlay. The note is stored in your [Notes](/platform/notes) area and can be linked to a project.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send into chat" icon="message-circle">
    Send the transcript as a chat message. The AI processes it immediately — useful for voice-prompted questions or Act mode tasks.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Tips for better transcription

<Tip>
Speak at a natural pace and pause briefly between separate ideas. Very fast speech or long run-on sentences can reduce transcription accuracy.
</Tip>

- **Speak clearly and at a normal pace.** Rushing or mumbling introduces more errors.
- **Pause between ideas.** A short pause helps the AI segment your thoughts into cleaner sentences.
- **Minimize background noise.** Noisy environments reduce accuracy. A quiet space or a headset microphone produces the best results.
- **State proper nouns carefully.** Names, product names, and technical terms are more likely to be misread. Review them in the transcript before saving.
- **Keep recordings focused.** Shorter, topic-specific recordings transcribe more cleanly than long, wide-ranging ones.

<Note>
Voice recording uses your browser's microphone API. No audio is stored permanently — only the resulting transcript text is kept when you choose to save or send it.
</Note>
