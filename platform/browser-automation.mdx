Browser automation gives Overlay the ability to control a real browser session on your behalf. Instead of describing what's on a website, Overlay can visit it directly — navigate pages, click buttons, fill in forms, scroll through results, and extract live information. You describe what you want done in plain language; the AI figures out how to do it in the browser.

## When to use browser automation

Use browser automation when you need the AI to work with live web content that isn't in your knowledge base or available through a connected integration.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fresh information" icon="globe">
    Browse live pages for current data — prices, news, job listings, or anything that changes frequently.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Interact with websites" icon="mouse-pointer">
    Fill out forms, click through multi-step flows, or interact with web apps that don't have an API.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Structured data extraction" icon="table">
    Scrape tables, lists, and structured content from pages for use in notes, sheets, or further analysis.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Web research" icon="search">
    Conduct multi-page research sessions — search, follow links, compare sources, and synthesize findings.
  </Card>

## Start a browser task

Browser automation runs inside **Act mode**. To trigger a browser task, switch to Act mode using the toggle in the chat input bar and describe what you want the browser to do.

You don't need any special syntax. Just describe the goal:

> "Go to the Hacker News front page and summarize the top 5 stories."

> "Search for 'product manager jobs in Berlin' on LinkedIn and list the first 10 results."

> "Visit our competitor's pricing page and pull the plan names and prices into a table."

Overlay determines whether a browser session is needed and launches one automatically.

<Info>
Browser tasks require Act mode. Switch from Ask to Act using the mode toggle before sending your message. Learn more in [Ask and Act modes](/platform/ask-and-act).
</Info>

## What a browser session looks like

Once Overlay starts a browser task, it narrates each action as it goes. You'll see a live feed of what the AI is doing: which URLs it's visiting, what it's clicking, what it's extracting, and any decisions it makes along the way.

The session narration looks like:

- "Opening the page at example.com…"
- "Scrolling to find the pricing table…"
- "Found 3 plan tiers — extracting names and prices…"
- "Returning results to the conversation."

This narration keeps you informed and makes it easy to spot if the AI has taken a wrong turn, so you can redirect it mid-session if needed.

## Follow-up actions in the same session

Browser sessions stay alive within a conversation. After the AI completes an initial task, you can ask follow-up questions or give additional instructions without starting a new session from scratch.

For example:

1. "List all the blog posts on their site from the last 3 months."
2. *(after results appear)* "Now open the most-shared post and pull the key points."
3. *(after that)* "Save all of this as a note titled 'Competitor Content Audit'."

Each follow-up builds on the same open session, so multi-step research or interaction flows work naturally.

## Limitations and safety notes

<Warning>
Browser automation acts on your behalf in a real browser. Review the AI's plan before it takes actions that are difficult to reverse — submitting forms, clicking confirmation buttons, or interacting with accounts you're logged into.
</Warning>

Keep these points in mind when using browser automation:

- **The AI acts on your behalf.** Any form submission or click happens in a real browser. If you're logged into an account, the AI can interact with it as you.
- **Verify before sensitive actions.** For anything involving payments, account changes, or publishing content, confirm the AI's interpretation of your request before it proceeds.
- **Dynamic pages may behave unexpectedly.** CAPTCHAs, login walls, and heavy JavaScript pages can interrupt a session. If a task gets stuck, restate your goal or try a different approach.
- **Sessions end with the conversation.** Browser sessions are scoped to a single chat thread. Starting a new conversation starts a fresh session.

<Tip>
For research tasks, ask Overlay to summarize what it found before taking any further action. This gives you a chance to confirm the AI is on the right track before it continues.
</Tip>
